Ride Responsibly

Good journeys start with good preparation.

Motorcycle journeys need planning, communication and sensible decisions. Passenger safety and comfort should always come first.

Before departure

  • ✓ Confirm the route, pickup and destination.
  • ✓ Discuss travel duration, breaks and weather.
  • ✓ Agree on luggage limits and passenger arrangements.
  • ✓ Clarify the total cost and what it covers.

During the ride

  • ✓ Use suitable, properly fitted helmets and protective gear.
  • ✓ Follow applicable traffic rules and passenger requirements.
  • ✓ Take breaks and communicate discomfort promptly.
  • ✓ Adjust or postpone travel when conditions are unsafe.

Ride requests are subject to availability, suitable conditions, applicable transport regulations and insurance requirements. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you hit the road.

What is a pillion ride companion?

A pillion passenger travels on the motorcycle behind the rider. OTX Ride Companion is intended for travellers interested in this style of motorcycle journey rather than a conventional car trip.

Can I request an intercity or long-distance ride?

You can enquire about your preferred route, including intercity and longer journeys. Each request needs to be reviewed for availability, feasibility, applicable rules and the arrangements required.

Can I choose my pickup point and departure time?

Share your preferred pickup location and approximate time. Flexibility can be discussed, but final arrangements depend on the route, schedule and other practical factors.

How much does a ride cost?

Cost depends on the journey and the agreed arrangements. Confirm the total cost, any applicable charges and what is included before agreeing to travel.

Can I travel with luggage?

Mention your luggage details in the enquiry. Motorcycle luggage capacity is limited, so suitability must be discussed and confirmed before departure.

Does submitting the form confirm my ride?

No. The form opens a WhatsApp message for discussion. Your ride is not confirmed until availability, arrangements, applicable requirements and costs have been agreed upon.
