Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire at any time?

Yes. You can send an enquiry any time, day or night. The requested departure time must be confirmed before the ride.

Can I ask for a ride today?

Yes, you can ask about same-day availability. It depends on schedule, daylight, weather, road conditions and route.

Is this a car or taxi service?

No. This page is for motorcycle pillion-ride enquiries. Please enquire only if you're comfortable travelling as a motorcycle passenger.

How much does a ride cost?

Confirm the total cost, stops, waiting time and return arrangements directly before agreeing to the ride.

Can I bring luggage?

Motorcycle storage is limited. Please mention luggage in advance so suitability can be discussed.
