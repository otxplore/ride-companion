Before you ride

Frequently asked questions

Can I book at any time?

You can send an enquiry at any time. Your requested ride time must be confirmed directly before the ride.

Can I request a ride for today?

Yes, you can ask about same-day availability. Confirmation depends on schedule, daylight, weather, road conditions and the route.

Is this a car or taxi service?

No. This page is for motorcycle pillion-ride enquiries. Please enquire only if you are comfortable travelling as a motorcycle passenger.

How much does it cost?

Confirm the total cost, stops, waiting time and return arrangements directly before agreeing to the ride.

Can I bring luggage?

Storage space is limited on a motorcycle. Please mention your luggage in advance so suitability can be discussed.
